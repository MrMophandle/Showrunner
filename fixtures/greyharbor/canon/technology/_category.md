## Check instructions

Technology facts are the exceptions that make a world rule survivable — the suit,
the collar, the field. Check them the strict way round: the rule holds unless the
artifact **shows** the exception in use. A character who owns a collar and left it
on the rack is not protected by owning it, and a check that reasons "he has one,
so presumably he wore it" is the failure mode this category exists to prevent.
