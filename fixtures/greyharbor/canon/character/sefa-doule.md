## Identity

- standing: recurring
- status: candidate
- aliases: the assessor

`status: candidate` is the only line that makes this sheet different from Ilse's,
and it is an instruction to the loader: register the identity, raise nothing.
Somebody proposes Sefa when Sefa is actually written into an episode, and Ryan
rules that promotion the same way he rules every other change to canon (D25).
Until then this file is a draft on disk, the database says `candidate`, and the
two agree.

## Relations

- species: unknown

Declared unknown, not left blank (D22). Sefa came in on a tender from somewhere
else and nobody at Grey Harbor has asked; `unknown` says a person looked and the
world has not decided, which is a different thing from a sheet nobody finished.
It is legal, it is tracked, and it satisfies the required `species` at
ratification — so promoting Sefa without answering the question is possible on
purpose.

## Body

Sefa arrived on the last scheduled tender of the quarter with a folding desk, a
line-office ledger, and instructions to put a number on a harbour that has not
moved cargo in four years. The instructions do not say what the number is for,
which Sefa has noticed and has not asked about.

The work is counting: berths, watts, hours of crew time, the cost of a beacon lit
every night against a lane with nothing in it. Sefa is good at it and is careful
never to be seen enjoying the place, on the theory that an assessor who likes a
harbour writes a report nobody upstairs believes.

## Facts

- Sefa Doule was sent by the line office to price Grey Harbor's remaining traffic
  and files against the line office's ledger, not the harbour's.
- Sefa arrived on the last scheduled tender of the quarter and has no berth
  assigned; the folding desk is set up in the dry stores.
- Sefa has asked Ilse for the harbour's power figures twice and been given the
  duty roster both times.
- Sefa has not told anyone at Grey Harbor what the assessment is for.
