# Ilse Renn

> Harbourmaster of Grey Harbor. Answers questions about the empty lane with a duty
> time, which she believes is an answer.

## Identity

- standing: core
- status: active
- aliases: the harbourmaster, Renn

## Relations

- species: Halvani

- carries: Kestrel-pattern containment collar

## Body

Ilse has kept Grey Harbor for eleven years, seven of them with traffic and four
without. She runs the harbour the way it was run when there were ships: the roster
goes up on Sunday, the quarterly return goes out on the ninth, and the beacon on
the Long Pier is lit every single night whether or not there is anything in the
lane to see it.

She is not sentimental about this and would be offended by the suggestion. Asked
why the beacon stays lit, she will tell you what it costs to run — a figure she
knows to the watt — and she will not tell you why the figure is worth paying. Ask
twice and she will find you something to do.

Her crew is four people. She would say she takes care of them. What she actually
does is spend them, in small amounts, on a harbour that has not been a harbour for
four years, and she has never once written that down.

## Facts

- Ilse has held the harbourmaster's post at Grey Harbor for eleven years.
- Ilse posts the duty roster on Sunday and treats it as the answer to most
  questions about the harbour.
- Ilse has lit the Long Pier beacon every night of the four years since the lane
  went quiet.
- Ilse knows the harbour's power budget to the watt and quotes it instead of
  giving reasons.
- Ilse has never filed a diversion against the harbour's spares ledger, though the
  spares have moved.
