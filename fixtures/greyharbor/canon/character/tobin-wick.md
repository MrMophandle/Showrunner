# Tobin Wick

> Dock rigger. Goes outside for a living, which is why what happens to him in
> episode 1 is a violation and not a stunt.

## Identity

- standing: core
- status: active
- aliases: Wick

## Relations

- species: Halvani
- stationed-at: Grey Harbor Station
- carries: Kestrel-pattern containment collar

## Body

Tobin came to Grey Harbor at nineteen to work a lane that had already stopped
carrying anything, and stayed because the work was outside and he was good at it.
He rigs the piers, services the berthing clamps, and keeps the relay housings
alive with parts that stopped being made before he was born.

He is careful in the way people who work in vacuum are careful — checklists,
habits, the same four movements in the same order every time — and the thing that
makes him useful to Ilse is that he will go and do a job before he has finished
deciding whether he should have said no. He has a collar. Everyone has a collar.

## Facts

- Tobin Wick has rigged Grey Harbor's piers for six years.
- Tobin is issued one of the station's four Kestrel-pattern collars and keeps it
  on the rack inside the No. 4 lock.
- Tobin has never refused a job Ilse asked for directly.
- Tobin services the Long Pier relay housings himself; nobody else on the station
  is rated for the work.
