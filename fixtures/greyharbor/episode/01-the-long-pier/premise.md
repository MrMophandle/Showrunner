# The Long Pier — premise

> Grey Harbor · Season 1 · Episode 1

A relay fails on the Long Pier and takes the beacon feed with it. Grey Harbor has
one spare, and it is committed to the water plant.

Ilse Renn takes it anyway, and sends Tobin Wick outside to fit it, and when he
asks her why the beacon matters she tells him what time he is on the roster. The
job gets done. The beacon comes back up over a lane that has been empty for four
years, and nobody writes down what it cost.

**Position:** *What the harbor is for* @ waypoint 2 — the harbour is worth
spending on. The spend is a part earmarked elsewhere and a man's morning, and Ilse
declines to itemise either.
