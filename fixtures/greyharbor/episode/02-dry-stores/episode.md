# Dry Stores

> The un-started episode: a number, a title, and nothing produced. It exists so
> every screen and every query has an honest empty case to render.

## Episode

- season: 1
- number: 2
- title: Dry Stores
- lifecycle: premise

## Arc positions

None. Episode 2 is **vanilla** — it touches no arc, which is legal, tracked, and
never a failure state (1.1). Half the reason it is in the fixture is that a season
where every episode sat on an arc would let a bug in `isVanilla` ship unnoticed,
and the season map would never render the case.

## Artifacts

None. Nothing has been written, so there is nothing on disk and no artifact rows —
not a premise-brief with an empty file, not a placeholder. An episode nobody has
started produces no artifacts, and the floor should say "not started" because it
is true, not because a row says so.

## Scenes

None, for the same reason. `num_scenes` is an output of a script that does not
exist yet.

## What this episode is about

The water plant's exchanger fails, three weeks after the harbourmaster took its
spare for the beacon. That is all that is decided. Nobody has written a premise
for it, and the fixture deliberately stops here — a fixture that pre-wrote every
episode would have nowhere for E4 to write.
