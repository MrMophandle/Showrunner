## What the branch built
`Canon/cast.json` is the one record of who has a sheet, a face and a voice (spec §1.2). The engine's `refs-ready` probe looks every subject up there (a face for everyone on the page, a LOCKED voice with its WAV for everyone who speaks, guests included) and names the one-line remedy when the index is absent — and since the fix wave (R-16) the `bible-ready` guard writes that same line two steps into a run, so an unmigrated show is refused before the writer is spent; every pipeline step takes the index as its input, the canon spine, the draft loop and `character-check` among them; the `registry-append` step that filled the character folders is gone. `scripts/lib/castindex.py` is the Python twin; the image generators condition on the reference and a gallery of at most three files named by the index and never list a folder. The prompt templates read the index first and only the sheets in play after it; the TTS script copies every voice from the index and designs none. Three migration tools build the index from the two registries (refusing on a path conflict), audit it against the retired repository read-only, and prune a character folder by moving everything but the sheet, the reference and three gallery images to `<productionDir>/_archive/characters/<Name>/`, under the path each file had inside the folder.

## Deferred
- **`checkGuestRefsDir` and `audio.guestRefsDir`** are still validated at load and read by `console/server/gates.ts` (the TTS-manifest picker); nothing in the engine reads them. The engine's own `guestRefsDir` is deleted, so `guestRefsDirOf` in the console is now the **only** implementation of the rule rather than a copy of one, and both doc blocks say so since F15. L2 retires them with the guest-refs convention. Cost if left: a dead key accepted by the loader, and a validation message that refuses a shape for the picker's sake rather than the probe's.
- **`audio.mainCast`** is still read by `validate-manifest.py` for the runway rule; the index could answer it (`debut` and `speaks`). Cost: one more hand-kept list.
- **The init scaffold** still writes both legacy registries — `Canon/refs.json` (`tools/templates/refs/canon-refs.json`) and `Production/voice-refs/refs.json` (`tools/templates/refs/voice-refs.json`) — and writes no `cast.json`; a new show gets its index from `cast-index-build.py --write` until L2 scaffolds one. **The prose is fixed** (F5): `tools/templates/show/README.md` now names `Canon/cast.json` as the record, lists the two registries as the files the build reads once, and gives the build as the remedy for the first stop; `tools/templates/canon/visual-style.md:24` asks its casting question against the index. **Cost, and it changed with R-16: a new show's first run now stops at `bible-ready`, its second step, not at `refs-ready`** — which is cheaper and is what `tools/test/init-exercise.test.ts` now asserts, but it is a different stop than this record described before the fix wave. Still describing the retired registries: `tools/templates/canon/readme.md:22` and `tools/templates/canon/voice-registry.md:3` (both call `Production/voice-refs/refs.json` the source of truth), and the two `tools/templates/refs/*.json` `_doc` strings. They are the scaffold's own files, so they go with L2's scaffold.
- **The Cast page** in the console's bible rail is L2.
- **`casting-gate.reject.md:6`** still sends a rejected manifest to `Canon/voice-registry.md` alone (L2 owns that gate).
- **The casting gate's voice re-pick is dead until Plan L2 moves it.** The spec kept it as "a re-pick of a filed voice" (§1.3), and it reads `<productionDir>/<episodeId>/guest-refs/` through `guestRefsDirOf`. Plan L1 stopped anything from putting candidate takes there: guests are filed centrally from their first episode, the TTS prompt copies their voice out of the index, and no step writes a per-episode take any more. So from L1 until L2, the pane at that gate shows **one quiet line and no choice** — `no candidate takes for this episode — the voices come from Canon/cast.json` (R-17) — for every episode whose guests are filed, which after Task 6 is every episode. **Cost, stated plainly: there is no way to re-pick a voice at a gate until L2 moves the picker to `Production/<episodeId>/casting/<slug>/`.** A voice is changed in the meantime by editing `Canon/cast.json` by hand. The gate itself still works and still approves; what is gone is the choice on it.
- **The Cast page cannot be served by the bible's file route** (the whole-branch review's note, and a correction to spec §1.4, which says it reads the index "through the bible's existing file route"). That route serves `BIBLE_FILES` keys and nothing else — `Canon/refs.json` is unreachable through it by name, and `console/test/bible.test.ts:340-347` pins that refusal — and `Canon/cast.json` is deliberately not a bible file (it is `NOT_THE_BIBLE` in `engine/test/episode-pipeline.test.ts`, because its completeness is the reference probe's report and not the bible's). **L2's Cast page needs its own read route**, or an explicit non-bible row on the existing one.
- **`tools/test/templates.test.ts`** pins nothing specific to the five templates Task 3 changed after H-19 went; the whole-corpus checks (renders, role line, a known canon file) are what remain. Cost: a regression in those templates is found by reading, as Task 3's review found "registry keys".
- **A build with an existing index** is no longer a gap. The index is the build's first source and the whole entry is deep-copied, so every field it holds survives — `name`, `debut`, `silent`, `status`, `face.gallery` — and since the fix wave (R-18) `kind`, `identity` and `baseline_prompt` are the index's as well: the visual bible fills one only where the index has none, and prints `kept from the index: <key>.<field>` where the two differ. Tested three ways: `debut` survives (Task 4's test), a hand-edited `kind` and `identity` survive a second `--write` and are reported, and a `baseline_prompt` deleted from the index is refilled from the bible. What is still untested by name: `silent` and a hand-edited `name`, which survive by the same deep copy. Cost: a merge-order change could drop those two unnoticed.
- **The audit's `epNN → s01eNN`** mapping is the retired repository's; `--retired` is not general.
- **The first real build** (dry run 2026-11-01: 25 entries, 0 conflicts) infers `character` for `ernie-grother` and `vanished` and `voice` for `narrator`, names `the-mute` "Mute" and the Iss-kar extras from their slugs, and holds no entry for Veleth (voice in the episode's guest-refs, no face): Task 6 hand-fixes the names, files Veleth's and Hallet's voices, and Veleth's face is L2's first real run (or a hand design before then — after L1 the probe demands it on his next appearance).
- **A freshly built index has `gallery: []` for everyone**, so the prune must be run with `--keep <key>=<file>,…` per crew member (or the index edited first) or it moves every image but the reference; nothing is deleted either way.
- **The `environment-check.md:29` and `outline.md:81` mentions of `Canon/characters/`** are sheet-path citations, not listings; left alone.
- **The show-name grep's word list is not the show's cast list.** It covers `dead ?light|deadlight|sarn|sable|opha|cricket|remo|trent|ilvaren|coalvane|the mute|ansa|mardo` and **does not cover `veleth`, `hallet` or `rourke`**, three names of the first show that this plan's own notes and Task 6 discuss. A green grep is therefore evidence about the words in the list and nothing more: a fixture or a comment naming one of those three would pass it. Fixtures are Harbor Lights (Vale, the Warden, Pim, Maeve, Harbor) by convention, which is what actually keeps the engine show-agnostic; the grep is the backstop, and the backstop has holes. Widening the list is a judgement for whoever next edits the constraint — every added word is a word no fixture may use.
- **`design-visual.py` renders the wrong kind of candidate for a character, twice over (Ryan-ruled 2026-11-01, spec §7).** It uses Qwen-Image, and character references must come from nano-banana; and it varies only the seed of one `baseline_prompt`, so its N candidates are one design in N poses (Hallet's three of 2026-10-31 and Veleth's three of 2026-11-01 both). Plan L2's `casting-design` replaces it for characters: one nano-banana call per candidate, each from its own brief written by `casting-brief`. Until then no character is cast from a Qwen render, and Hallet's locked Qwen reference is regenerated before his next episode renders. Cost of leaving it: every new character's face would be a Qwen look-alike of itself, and s02e01's Hallet shots would condition on a reference the ruling retires.
