Ferro

“the dockman” active standing · recurring species · Hab-born human
character · standing is declared intent; appearances below are computed from provenance · species is required on every character — its facts load with him into every check

Appearance

Built like the work found him early. The rust-brown dock jacket in every light; a dock-suit worn half unsealed indoors, as if the outside never quite lets go of him.

Voice & manner

Gravel kept low. Says numbers the way other men say names. Enters rooms without knocking because docks don’t knock.

History

Nine years on the Meridian Spur docks; before that, nothing he offers. Knows hull-frost by sound. Trusts ledgers over stations and Vessa over ledgers — lately, and not all the way.
