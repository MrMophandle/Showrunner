The beacon

story · season 1 at waypoint 3 of 4
4 waypoints · touched by 5 episodes · last landed in ep05, in flight now
What this arc is
A signal has been repeating out of the drift since before the settlement was built. The crew hears it, traces it, answers it — and discovers the answer was never a distress call. It was bait, and it has been working for nine years. The question the arc asks: what do you owe a voice that was never asking for help?
