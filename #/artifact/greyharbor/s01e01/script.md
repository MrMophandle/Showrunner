# The Long Pier — script

> Grey Harbor · Season 1 · Episode 1 · draft 1

## 1 · INT. GREY HARBOR STATION — MESS DECK — 06:10

> Ilse tells Tobin the pier relay is dead. The lane has been empty four years and
> the quarterly is in nine days.

The mess deck is warm, which on Grey Harbor is a complaint. ILSE RENN is at the
end of the long table with a slate and the night's log. TOBIN WICK comes in
sideways, still pulling an arm through his coveralls.

ILSE
Pier relay went dark at two.

TOBIN
Which one.

ILSE
Four. The beacon feed runs through it.

Tobin sits down opposite her and looks at the log without picking it up.

TOBIN
Nothing's come up the lane since Marrowmas. Nothing's coming up it in nine days
either.

ILSE
Quarterly's in nine days.

TOBIN
That's not the same sentence.

ILSE
It's the one I've got.

She turns the slate around. The relay's fault line is a single flat trace where a
signal should be.

## 2 · INT. HARBOURMASTER'S OFFICE — 06:40

> Ilse pulls the station's last spare relay — the one committed to the water
> plant — and answers Tobin's challenge with the roster.

Two decks up, and colder. Ilse has the spares drawer open. There is one relay in
it, wrapped, with a plant tag knotted through the wrapper.

TOBIN
That's the plant's.

ILSE
It's the station's.

TOBIN
It's got their tag on it.

Ilse cuts the tag off with a thumbnail and puts the relay on the desk between
them. She does not put the tag anywhere. It stays in her hand.

TOBIN (CONT'D)
Ilse. If the plant's exchanger goes and there's no spare —

ILSE
Roster says you're on the pier at seven.

Tobin looks at her for a moment. Then he picks up the relay.

TOBIN
Roster says I'm on the pier at seven.

## 3 · INT. NO. 4 LOCK — 07:05

> Tobin kits out at the lock and cycles it. Four collars stay on the rack.

The lock is a grey box with a counter over the outer door. The collar rack is
bolted to the inboard wall.

Tobin takes a torque bar down off the tool rack and turns it over once in his
hands, the way he does. He puts the wrapped relay in his hip pocket. Behind him,
four Kestrel collars hang closed on their pegs.

He keys the cycle. The counter starts at ninety.

TOBIN
(to the counter)
Ninety seconds of nothing.

He watches it go.

## 4 · EXT. THE LONG PIER — 07:07

> Tobin works the relay housing at the head of the pier, in his coveralls, for
> three minutes.

The pier runs out from the ring in a long spar of gantry and clamp, black against
black, the housings spaced along it like knuckles. The head of it is four hundred
metres out.

Tobin comes out onto the pier in his coveralls and goes down the spar hand over
hand, torque bar hooked at his belt.

At the head, he gets the housing open. The dead relay is a scorched brick. He
works it out of its seat, seats the new one, and drives the four bolts down in the
order he always drives them.

Three minutes of it, start to finish.

The handset in the housing has a light on it. He keys it twice — two clicks, the
answer he always gives — and starts back down the spar.

## 5 · INT. HARBOURMASTER'S OFFICE — 07:20

> Tobin brings the dead relay in. Ilse doesn't ask what it cost, and doesn't
> write down what she spent.

Tobin comes in with the scorched relay and puts it on the desk. Ilse is watching
the feed trace come back up, a slow green climb.

TOBIN
Plant's going to notice, eventually.

ILSE
Then they'll tell me.

TOBIN
That's not a plan, that's a queue.

Ilse doesn't answer. The plant's tag is on the desk where she finally put it down,
and she sets a slate on top of it without appearing to notice she has.

ILSE
Beacon'll want the housing panel closed before dark cycle.

TOBIN
It's closed.

ILSE
It wants checking.

## 6 · EXT. THE LONG PIER — CONTINUOUS

> Ilse checks the housing panel herself, and the beacon comes up. The lane is empty.

Ilse is at the head of the pier in a hardsuit, helmet sealed, one glove flat on
the relay housing. She works the catch a quarter turn down and lets go of it.

Out at the head of the mast the beacon comes up — one long white pulse, then dark,
then again.

She stands there for a while, in the only place on Grey Harbor where you can see
the whole lane at once.

There is nothing in it.

FADE OUT.
