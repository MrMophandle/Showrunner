# The Long Pier — outline

> Grey Harbor · Season 1 · Episode 1

Three movements. Deliberately not a scene list: the writer breaks the scenes, and
the count is whatever the script turns out to need (D3).

## Morning, inboard

Ilse tells Tobin the No. 4 relay went dark in the night and took the beacon feed
with it. Tobin says what everyone on the station says — nothing has come up the
lane since Marrowmas — and Ilse answers with the quarterly, which is not the same
sentence and is the only one she offers.

Upstairs, the spares drawer has exactly one relay in it, tagged for the water
plant. Ilse cuts the tag off. Tobin names the risk out loud: if the plant's
exchanger goes there is nothing to put in it. Ilse tells him what time he is on
the roster. He takes the relay.

The audience should be able to feel that a decision was made here, and should not
be able to point to a sentence where Ilse admits one.

## The pier

Tobin kits out at the No. 4 lock and cycles it, and goes out along the spar to the
head of the pier to change the relay. The work itself is routine and he is good at
it — this is the part of the job he came here for.

He is out there long enough for the length of it to register.

## After

Tobin brings the dead relay back to the office. The feed trace climbs green behind
Ilse while he tells her the plant will notice eventually, and she says they will
tell her when they do. The plant's tag is still on her desk. She does not throw it
away and she does not write anything down.

The last image is Ilse alone at the head of the pier with the beacon up over her:
one long white pulse, then dark, then again — and the whole lane visible from
there, with nothing in it.
