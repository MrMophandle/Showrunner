# Continuity ledger

## How to use
This file is the running record of what HAPPENED, episode by episode, and it is written by the pipeline rather than by hand: after every episode the `propose` step appends to the three sections below — a new entry under `## Episode log`, new or amended threads under `## Open threads`, and anything the episode closed moved down to `## Resolved`. You edit what it wrote at the canon gate, where the diff is shown before anything is committed. Keep plot state here and keep descriptions on the entity sheets under `Canon/characters/`, `Canon/locations/` and `Canon/factions/`: it is the plot state, not the descriptions, that a writing agent most often contradicts.
